STRICT COMPLIANCE & SAFETY DECLARATION

Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) & CSAM Prevention Standards

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

Platform: NightOwl: AfterHours (Operated by ArkArk Interactive)

1. Absolute Zero-Tolerance Policy

NightOwl maintains an absolute, uncompromising zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), grooming, solicitation of minors, or any underage activity. NightOwl is strictly designed and restricted for consenting adults aged 18 years and older.

2. Age Restriction & Verification Safeguards

  • Strict 18+ Barrier: All users must explicitly self-certify being 18+ years of age prior to account onboarding.
  • Biometric Liveness AI Checks: Client-side 2.5-second camera sweep verification ensures real adult liveness without storing facial imagery.
  • Immediate Account Banning: Any account suspected of being operated by or targeting an individual under 18 is permanently terminated and blacklisted immediately.

3. Proactive Detection, Moderation & Reporting

  • In-App Reporting: Every chat session features an always-accessible reporting tool specifically categorizing suspected underage violations and non-consensual media.
  • 7-Day Moderation Review Vault: Media is held in an internal encrypted admin vault for 7 days to facilitate safety review, audit against abuse, and support lawful enforcement requests before permanent hard-purging.
  • Mandatory Law Enforcement Reporting: NightOwl complies with all national and international statutory reporting obligations, including reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and Indian law enforcement authorities under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.
