NightOwl Account Deletion & Data Erasure Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Platform: NightOwl: AfterHours (Operated by ArkArk Interactive)

1. Right to Erasure & Deletion

In accordance with the Google Play Data Safety Policy and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, all NightOwl users have the unequivocal right to request permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

2. In-App Deletion (Instant)

Users can delete their account instantly inside the NightOwl mobile application:

  1. Tap the Profile Icon in the top navigation bar.
  2. Scroll down to the Account Deletion & Data Purge section.
  3. Tap "Delete My Account Permanently" and confirm.

3. Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit your moniker or account identifier below to initiate manual deletion:

✓ Deletion request registered. Your profile and records will be purged within 24 hours.

4. What Data Gets Permanently Purged?

  • Profile Data: Moniker, gender, age, desire tags, midnight vibes, and avatar.
  • Chat Messages & Vault Media: All active sessions, burned transcripts, and temporary media vault items.
  • Coin Ledger: Coin balance, rewards, and referral associations.
  • Matches & Radar Signals: Active matches and location proximity indexes.

5. Data Retention Exceptions

No personal profile data is retained following deletion. Minimal server access logs (de-identified IP addresses) are retained for a maximum of 30 days solely for security audit purposes and statutory compliance under Indian IT Rules, after which they are automatically expunged.
